How the Replay System Works

The AMS2 Auto Director can automatically switch cameras during your recorded game replays! To do this, it needs a Timeline Log of all the interesting events that happened during the race (overtakes, accidents, close battles).

1. Start your live race (or a multiplayer stream).
2. Click Start Capture below. Leave this page open in the background.
3. The logger will silently record all overtakes and crashes into a timeline.
4. When the race ends, click Stop Capture & Download to save your `.txt` log.
5. After the race, load the AMS2 Replay, open the Auto Director Python App, click "Load Replay Log", and the director will automatically focus on the action as the replay plays back!
